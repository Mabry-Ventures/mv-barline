Support Barline.

Whether something is not working or Barline is working well enough that you want to help keep it free, you are in the right place.

Get help or report a problem.

Start with the troubleshooting guide for permissions, hidden items, and display setup. If the problem remains, open a public issue with your Barline version, macOS version, display setup, and clear reproduction steps.

Report an issue

Review screenshots and diagnostics before attaching them. Do not include passwords, payment details, or private menu bar contents.

Make an optional contribution.

Barline is free and open source. Every feature is included, with no account, subscription, advertising, or payment reminders. If it is useful to you, a one-time contribution helps fund development and macOS compatibility testing.

Contribute via Stripe

Choose your amount in USD. One-time support for Mabry Ventures LLC, with no features or support priority to unlock.
