For Apple Silicon · macOS 26 and macOS 27

Less clutter. Still one click away.

Barline gives you control over a crowded menu bar without turning organization into another job.

Choose what stays visible.

Organize items into visible, hidden, and always-hidden sections, with control over spacing.

Reveal items when needed.

Open the three-dot control to reach hidden items in a shelf, then let them quietly rehide.

Find items from the keyboard.

Search your menu bar locally instead of hunting through a row of icons.

Save useful layouts.

Capture an arrangement once, restore it later, and keep recovery tools close if an app or display changes.

Let Focus set the scene.

Connect a saved layout to a native macOS Focus Filter so work and personal setups can stay distinct.

Adapt across displays.

Use display-specific layouts and contextual rules while Barline keeps its menu bar information on your Mac.
