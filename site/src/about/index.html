A quieter menu bar.
A clear purpose.

Barline is a native Mac utility from Mabry Ventures LLC. It keeps the menu bar organized without accounts, subscriptions, advertising, or payment gates.

Reliability comes first.

Our priority is the everyday experience: quick access to hidden items, dependable recovery, and layouts that work with macOS Focus. New features earn their place through testing, not novelty. Every feature is free; supporting the project is optional.

Built on open-source work.

Barline is derived from Ice, originally created by Jordan Baird, and the macOS compatibility fork maintained by Xinyan Lu. We are grateful to them and the contributors whose work made this foundation possible.

Barline is an independent project. Ice and its contributors do not endorse Barline. Original copyright notices and authorship are preserved in the repository and its Git history.
