## Verified defect and repair

The occurrence counter previously used bundle plus numeric-normalized title.
Distinct strong Accessibility identifiers therefore changed full identity when
geometry order changed or a sibling disappeared. Saved assignments still used
old aliases; retained merging added those identities beside live replacements,
which could fail bundle-level concealment visible. Regression tests reproduce
the old behavior and verify a structured complete normalized identity bucket.

Legacy migration permits only occurrence-alias changes with identical bundle,
strong AX identifier, title and fingerprint. Both authoritative saved identities
and current live identities must be unique. Conflicting app groups remain
visible and noneditable without overwriting their saved intent or disabling
unrelated discovery. Absent items retain intent; historical metadata never proves
live ownership. Sections, ranks, profile groups and spacer anchors are preserved.
Profile archives are resolved at point of use, not rewritten wholesale.

The migrated state has a bounded single-key authority record for assignments,
remembered sections and sanitized descriptors. Partial writes of legacy mirrors
cannot split a hidden assignment from its metadata. Present invalid authority
does not fall back to stale mirrors. Normal commits and inventory refreshes keep
the authority record current; complete proposed state is validated before native
mutation. This is one process-visible verified preference value, not a claim
that UserDefaults supplies an fsync-backed filesystem transaction.

Up to four distinct damaged records are quarantined before restoring a verified
complete backup. A full quarantine fails closed without deleting prior evidence.
Read-only shelf ordering, concealment canonicalization and conflict isolation
preserve the original observation timestamp, generation and menu-tracking flag.
Search favorites/aliases and profile/group membership resolve at point of use;
the complete stored/live identity families are checked before accepting a match.

Strict workspace/temporary-reveal checkpoint identity checks are preserved.
Unresolved legacy checkpoints require explicit recovery and are not reported as
successfully restored. Migration cannot grant cross-display mutation authority.
